Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information SpecIt: Maintenance Tracker ("SpecIt", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the SpecIt iOS app and this website.

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address and a password, or the name and email address shared by Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • What you track: the homes, vehicles, appliances, instruments, documents and payments you add, including brand, model, serial and VIN numbers, specifications, maintenance tasks, due dates and notes.
  • Location you enter: the ZIP code in your profile and any ZIP codes you add to a property. We do not collect your device's GPS location.
  • Photos you choose to scan: when you photograph or upload an equipment label, the image is sent for processing so the details can be read and filled in. We do not keep the photo after it has been processed.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage records: how many scans and online part searches you've used (to apply free limits), and which product links you tap (to measure partner referrals).
  • Pro requests: the job details, ZIP code and pros you chose when you request a quote.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To run the app: store your items, schedule reminders, and show the right parts for your models.
  • To read equipment labels and look up model-specific parts.
  • To find and contact local service pros when you ask us to.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and keep the service secure.
  • To improve SpecIt's shared parts database. When you confirm a part fits a model, that model-to-part match (not your identity) may help other users.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that run SpecIt for us: Supabase (database and hosting) and Anthropic (reading label photos and looking up parts). They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Thumbtack, only when you request a quote: we send the job description, equipment details and the property's ZIP code to the pros you select. If you connect your Thumbtack account, Thumbtack's own privacy policy applies to that account.
  • Amazon and other retailers: when you tap a product link, you leave SpecIt for the retailer's site. Links may contain an affiliate tag that lets the retailer credit SpecIt for a purchase; we do not send your personal information to the retailer.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or SpecIt.
  • Business transfers: if SpecIt is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Calendar and notifications

If you allow it, SpecIt adds reminders to your device calendar and shows notifications. These stay on your device; we do not read your other calendar events.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete items at any time in the app. To delete your account and its data, use the delete-account option in the app's settings or email us at getspecit@gmail.com; we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

Security

Your data is stored with access controls so that only your account can read it, and is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

SpecIt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request; we won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date. If changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
